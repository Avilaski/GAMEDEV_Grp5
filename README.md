# Bomberman Rhythm Ultra
Made by Adrian Patrick Bacolod, Christen Cielo Infante, Greg Avila Jr., and Gianela Kim Agsalon

## About this game
Bomberman Rhythm Ultra is a rhythm game based off the retro action game Bomberman. Taking inspiration from Bomberman's core mechanics of planting bombs to defeat enemies and gaining powerups from crates, this game turns them into a rhythm-based gameplay accompanied by the original game's soundtrack. In Bomberman Rhythm Ultra, Bomberman defeats enemies by planting bombs in time with the beat, or he risks taking damage if the bomb misses them by exploding too early or too late. The crates, meanwhile, contains heals that Bomberman has to claim before they fall off the conveyor belts and break. Bomberman wins if he makes it to the end of the song without losing all his HP.


## Controls
**A** - Move to first (top/left) lane  
**S** - Move to second (middle) lane  
**D** - Move to third (bottom/right) lane  
**Spacebar** - Plant a bomb
